## Exact local-model selection

The bridge requires a catalog match, a minimal completion probe, gateway health schema v2 and an exact-model public-tunnel health check.

The refresh performs Python/methodology preflight, exact-model bridge verification, candidate/SEC extraction, the v2.1.2 market/SEC report, order-evidence reconciliation, the initial seven-field report, live source federation, diversified System operationalization, the final signed snapshot and signed sync/model-route maintenance.

## Serenity public-logic standard

The project is labelled **Serenity public-logic high-fidelity reconstruction**. It does not claim an official Serenity formula, endorsement or reproduction of a private process. Legacy `serenity_*` machine keys remain only for compatibility and are displayed as **System operationalization** fields.

Evidence Standard v3 forbids keyword, sector, high-margin, revenue-growth, beta, short-interest or price-action proxies from independently proving a bottleneck. A positive bottleneck claim requires an evidence-bound graph edge touching the focal company, at least two independent primary/corroborating publisher families, at least one primary family and at least one family independent of the focal issuer. Gross margin alone cannot prove replacement friction; revenue growth alone is capped at 40% of TAM-capture points; issuer-only commercial statements are provisional; conflicting primary material values fail closed. A severe high-quality primary thesis killer may invalidate a positive thesis asymmetrically.

Different URLs, mirrors, syndication and identical content from one publisher count once. Different issuers filing through SEC may be independent publishers, and a counterparty filing may corroborate a focal issuer. Search snippets and social reposts do not create independent proof.

## Live source federation

The reviewed catalog currently contains 101 source entries, but catalog size is an inventory—not a claim that all 101 were used. Each run writes an auditable live federation snapshot that records actual successful families, per-ticker families, concentration and unresolved conflicts.

Required live families are SEC EDGAR, Nasdaq Trader Symbol Directory, World Bank indicators, U.S. BLS public data and ECB SDMX. GLEIF provides optional independent legal-entity identity. Yahoo/yfinance remains a non-authoritative T3 candidate/market observation. Alpha Vantage is an optional second market-observation family when the owner supplies an API key; without it, Yahoo-only market confidence stays degraded.

The gate requires at least five successful global families, at least four official families, at least 80% of Top20 tickers with two independent families and two official identity/filing families, no unresolved material conflict and no source family above the 65% evidence concentration cap. Claim scopes remain separate: listing identity does not prove revenue, macro data does not prove issuer orders, and a filing does not prove market price.

## Activation

The canonical `activate-v213-seven-field-schedule.ps1` validates the fresh source federation, final diversified scoring version, transparent multi-source evidence and exact Top20/report order before any Worker mutation. Only then does it call the isolated-Wrangler, exact-prior-version rollback core. Successful activation installs a stable runtime and 07:20/20:20 local refreshes ahead of the 08:00/21:00 LINE pushes.

Machine field keys remain stable English identifiers; `config/field-labels.zh-en.json` supplies Traditional Chinese and English display labels. The packaging workflow never performs Production deployment.
