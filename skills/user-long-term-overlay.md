# User-defined long-term suitability overlay

> Attribution: repository-owner preference.
>
> This is not a Serenity view, not a Leopold Aschenbrenner view, and not part of either source's original methodology.

## Purpose

After the report faithfully presents source-derived views and the repository's separate research score, this overlay annotates which companies appear more suitable for the user's preferred holding horizon of at least two years.

It must not alter:

- source quotations or paraphrases;
- source-specific time horizons;
- methodology attribution;
- the system's primary research score;
- evidence records.

## Assessment dimensions

The overlay evaluates only factors relevant to surviving a multi-year holding period:

1. **Demand durability** — whether demand is structural rather than a short-lived event.
2. **Moat durability** — qualification cycles, switching costs, intellectual property, capacity control and credible substitutes.
3. **Financial endurance** — balance sheet, free cash flow, liquidity and ability to fund expansion without repeated dilution.
4. **Dilution and refinancing risk** — ATM issuance, stock-based compensation, debt maturity and capital intensity.
5. **Customer concentration** — dependence on one customer, contract duration and counterparty quality.
6. **Cyclicality** — commodity exposure, inventory cycles and sensitivity to a single product transition.
7. **Evidence strength** — filings, named contracts, customer confirmation and dated capacity disclosures.
8. **Valuation resilience** — whether the thesis depends on continued multiple expansion rather than operating delivery.
9. **Disconfirmation conditions** — explicit facts that would invalidate the long-term thesis.

## Output labels

- **較適合長期研究** — durable thesis and adequate financial/evidence support.
- **條件式長期觀察** — potentially durable, but one or more material conditions require monitoring.
- **偏事件／交易型** — thesis depends mainly on a dated catalyst, short squeeze, temporary shortage or near-term repricing.
- **資料不足** — insufficient primary evidence or financial data to assess a multi-year holding period.

## Required report wording

Every use of this overlay must include:

> This long-term suitability annotation reflects the repository user's preference for a holding horizon of at least two years. It is not attributed to Serenity or Leopold Aschenbrenner and is not a return forecast or trade instruction.

## Separation rule

A source may express a long-term or short-term view on a particular company. The report must preserve that company-specific horizon in the source-view section. The user overlay is then shown separately and may agree or disagree with the source view, with reasons.
