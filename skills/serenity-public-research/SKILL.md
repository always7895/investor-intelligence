# 公開研究 / Public research

## 必守邊界 / Non-negotiable boundaries

- Only public **Serenity / @aleabitoreddit** material; `@stockgodserenity` is a different, quarantined identity. Never claim an official score, private process, portfolio or advice.
- Source views are attributable opinions, not company facts. Prove company facts independently; deduplicate mirrors, forks and shared archive lineage.
- Refresh direct original sources before asserting a current ticker view. Archive-only material remains unverified; stale views must not be presented as current.
- No owner/broker/private-data fallback, trading, paid fallback, invented order totals or silent source downgrade. Missing evidence never upgrades LIMITED.

## 執行與歸因 / Execution and attribution

A SKILL.md on disk, or its path in a policy, is not proof that Pi discovered/read/executed it. Before claiming skill-backed research, record the Pi provider/model, loaded skill/reference hashes and actual public-source tool evidence. Deterministic pipeline output alone is not an agent research receipt.

Choose the output lane explicitly:
- **ATTRIBUTED_SOURCE_VIEWS / 本人公開觀點**: require retrieved original @aleabitoreddit content, exact supporting passage, publication/as-of time, ticker identity, stance, horizon and later revisions. Mention/watch/positive thesis/disclosed position are different; none automatically means a current holding. If originals are unavailable or stale, return UNVERIFIED/UNAVAILABLE, not a generic replacement Top20.
- **SYSTEM_RESEARCH_CANDIDATES / 系統研究候選**: independently researched companies, explicitly not her endorsement/watchlist. Preserve company-evidence and LIMITED gates even when her view is verified.
- **COMPARISON / 差異核對**: use one declared as-of cutoff; show overlap and per-ticker exclusion stage (universe discovery, market/exchange coverage, data, evidence or ranking), contrary/revised views and unknowns. A missing candidate in the seed universe cannot be recovered by changing final weights. Do not hard-code her tickers or optimize weights merely to imitate her list.

Methodology resemblance does not guarantee identical selections. An unverified account/source, generic market screener bias or an unexecuted skill is an implementation/evidence gap to disclose, not an excuse to claim fidelity.

## 使用方式 / Workflow

For substantive research, **read [RESEARCH_METHOD.md](references/RESEARCH_METHOD.md) completely before producing conclusions**, then read [CROSS_VALIDATION.md](references/CROSS_VALIDATION.md) for economic bridges, independent source capabilities, reconciliation and evaluation bias. The original methodology remains intact; this entrypoint is not a replacement for its evidence rules.

1. System change → constrained layer → companies, not ticker-first promotion.
2. Separate relationship evidence from scarcity and hard dependency.
3. Distinguish nominal qualified suppliers from effective current-ramp substitutes.
4. Treat qualification/commitments as forward evidence, not realized revenue.
5. Separate operating thesis from equity capture and financing severity.
6. Preserve dated source-view lifecycle and independent source families.
7. Report multi-axis confidence; label claims SUPPORTED, INFERENCE or withheld UNSUPPORTED.
8. Separate timing from price appreciation; end with explicit falsifiers.
